StudyBroker: a demo for Inside the Broker

This is the dashboard for the study broker from the book Inside the Broker by Charles Vance. Here it is driven by a simulated broker running in your browser: an edge node, a primary host, a member's client and the Sparkplug tap, all talking MQTT 3.1.1 and Sparkplug B, with every packet in the trace in the book's own format. Nothing connects to a real broker, and reloading the page starts it over.
